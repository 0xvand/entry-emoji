## Why it should win

Every other entrant will send the island, the beach, the palm tree, the
plane — postcards of a place with nobody in it. The hut is the one pick
that is a place *and* a stay: a thatched bungalow on the shore, the
specific dream of waking up already there, barefoot, with nothing on the
schedule. It names a dream vacation in a single glyph — shelter, shore
and slow mornings at once — the way the brief's own hammock does, but as
a whole scene rather than a prop. It is vivid at a glance, personal
rather than generic, and fresh without being obscure: one codepoint
(U+1F6D6), no joiners, no variation selector, clear at any size.

## Run

`python3 main.py` writes the emoji to `/output/entry.txt` (override the
path with the `ENTRY_OUTPUT` environment variable for local testing).
